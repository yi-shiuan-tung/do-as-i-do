Scope and limitations

Detailed fitting metrics · Tracking provenance

234 sampled frames (120–819) from pipette_depth_03, played at 10 fps. Original HaWoR is on the left; TAPIR-guided reconstruction is in the middle; synchronized recorded RGB is on the right. Both 3D views share camera controls. Hands without usable observations are hidden.

Persistent TAPIR points track both hands and both objects. Points are associated with mesh triangles using fixed barycentric coordinates. Their 2D motion predicts the next articulated hand poses and rigid object poses; each frame is then corrected jointly against masks, measured RealSense depth, and WiLoR RGB landmarks. Confidence, segmentation consistency, and surface association gate tracks. Fits propagate forward and backward from frame 549; initialization and reappearing hands use the previous soft RGB-D fit. Tracking uses short context segments, but there is no overlapping-window pose refinement.

All six mesh pairs receive bidirectional soft collision penalties with a 0.25 mm tolerance. There is no hard penetration bound, inferred contact, contact attraction, or contact persistence. Residual intersections can remain. Hand self-collisions and motion between samples are not constrained. Hand shape and calibrated camera are fixed. Depth errors measure visible overlap only; lower errors do not alone establish better reconstruction.
