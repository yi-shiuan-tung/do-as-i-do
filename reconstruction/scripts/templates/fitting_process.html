What is replayed, and what is not?

RGB, SAM3 masks, aligned depth, TAPIR point tracks, and final mesh poses are saved results. Predicted poses are reconstructed from each saved motion transform and depth increment using the same prediction function as the fitter. No segmentation, tracking, or fitting is rerun here. Individual optimizer iterations were not saved; panels 5 and 6 show its input and final result.

Fitting begins at the reference frame, then proceeds forward and backward. On the backward pass, the previously fitted frame is later in video time. Playback is slowed to 4 fps for inspection and advances in video order; the transition label always shows the actual fitting direction.

Pose panels show the complete mesh through occluding hands and scene objects for inspection. The reported IoU uses the fitter’s occlusion handling, so it is not the overlap of these unmasked preview overlays. Oblique views share one camera and show camera-space geometry, not world registration. The tube’s axial rotation and transparent-surface depth remain uncertain; hand–object contact is not enforced.
